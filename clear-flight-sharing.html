Flight Sharing

Sharing a flight out of the CLEAR app as a rich card that answers “when do I pick you up?” before it’s asked.

Company
Timeframe2026
PlatformiOS

Context

Someone texts “What time should I meet you at the airport?” The answer lives in the CLEAR app — flight number, arrival time, terminal, gate — but getting it into the conversation meant a screenshot or retyping it by hand. Flight Sharing sends it as a card instead.

Problem

Travel details are the most-asked-for and least-shareable thing in the app. A screenshot loses the live status the moment the flight moves, and typing it out drops exactly the details — terminal, gate — that the person picking you up actually needs.

Process

The shared card carries the flight’s identity and its logistics together: carrier and flight number (DL 707), the route as the headline (JFK → DEN), and the arrival time, terminal, and gate as supporting detail. The CLEAR mark sits on the card so the recipient knows where the information came from and that it is live.

User Journey

A traveler opens their flight in CLEAR, shares it into a thread, and the recipient sees a formatted card rather than a wall of text — enough to plan a pickup without a follow-up question.
